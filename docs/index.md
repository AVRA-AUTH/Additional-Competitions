# Hello, Copernicus Users.
---

This page contains documentation for our user-friendly GUI for planning an ASV route. The route is then simulated, based on custom functional software, to collect currently synthetic chlorophyll data, while cross-checking with the Copernicus database.

The software, as a finished product, would have multiple applications:

- It is directed to owners of coastal farms who wish to collect or simply validate chlorophyll data around their properties
- It is complementary to the Copernicus database, as it enriches the information contained on targeted areas of the grid
